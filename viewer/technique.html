The controlled comparison

Each scene was sampled once:50 steps,1536×672,243frames,24fps, BF16 H3 and reviewed360 LoRA1.0. Its exact saved video latent was decoded natively and with both context widths. Compare the same frame, viewing direction and75° horizontal field of view.

Two positive examples establish a useful improvement, not universal seamlessness. Pixel boundary mismatch is a diagnostic; inspect structure, detail and temporal stability too. Camera motion is only a requirement when requested.
